The Hostel and PG Management System is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline the management of hostels and paying guest accommodations. It offers a dual login feature for students and rent owners, enhancing the user experience for both parties. Leveraging AI technology, the system aids students in finding the best PG options tailored to their needs, complete with detailed reviews and ratings. Room allocation and management are efficiently handled, ensuring a seamless living experience.

Key features include an intuitive rent due system and secure online rent payment options, facilitating hassle-free financial transactions. The system supports online booking, allowing students to either book rooms directly or schedule visits. Enquiry and booking options provide flexibility for prospective tenants. A robust complaint section ensures that both students and rent owners can address issues promptly, fostering a healthy living environment.

The platform includes feedback management to capture and analyze student experiences, enhancing service quality. It offers the option to indicate food requirements, providing an added layer of convenience. Detailed area information helps students familiarize themselves with their new surroundings, while an AI-powered nearest PG finder simplifies the search process.

Advanced search and filter options enable students to narrow down their choices based on specific criteria. The system also features booking and reservation management, referral discounts, and optional social features to build a community for outsider students. This holistic approach ensures an efficient, user-friendly, and engaging experience for all users involved in hostel and PG management.
